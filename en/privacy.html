Privacy Policy

Updated: November 1, 2026 · Version 2.2

Overview

By default, records stay on this device. iCloud sync and family sharing are optional. After separate consent, the baby avatar and notes on basic care records can also sync; family members can see them. Limited snapshots do not include avatars or notes. Health facts, growth measurements, diaries, handover messages, journal photos and thumbnails stay on the device. Private cloud photos and family cloud albums are not available.

1. Storage, sync and sharing

When you choose a separate iCloud account space and reopen the app as directed, permitted new content enters the Apple CloudKit sync queue. Existing local records, photos and drafts are not automatically migrated. Permitted content includes the baby’s nickname, birthday and optional sex; structured times, amounts, durations and fixed sleep states for feeding, sleep, diapers, pumping, solid foods, baths and dryness checks; and limited snapshots of those structured fields. A queue entry is not a cloud receipt.

Baby avatars and notes on basic care records stay local unless you separately enable “Avatar and record notes sync”. They may then be uploaded and visible to family members. Notes may contain health information; the app does not filter keywords, so do not enter anything you do not want to share. Health facts and their notes, growth measurements, diary text, handover messages, journal photos, thumbnails, photo allowance records and drafts remain local.

Family care sharing is separate from family photo albums. Anyone who obtains a valid invitation link, including a forwarded link, may join with an Apple account after Apple confirmation; the owner need not name the account beforehand. Members can read, add, edit and delete shared records. Share links only with trusted people. The app uses CloudKit private or invited shared databases, not a public database or a developer-operated server. The developer cannot read baby content in your private iCloud database. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its services.

2. Children and other people

The app is intended for adult parents and authorized caregivers, not for independent use by children. Record a child’s information only with parental authority or guardian consent. Children’s information, health records and some photos may be sensitive. Record only what is needed; obtain authorization before recording another person’s information.

3. Information and purposes

Information you enter, including profile details, feeding, sleep, care, measurements, health facts, diaries, photos and handovers, supports on-device recording, review and statistics. Drafts, photo usage and reminder settings support their respective features. Permitted cloud fields and separately authorized avatars and care notes support your chosen sync and family sharing. The app currently uses no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party behavior analytics or crash reporting, and does not sell baby information to advertisers.

4. Photos and permissions

Journal photos and thumbnails stay local. The baby avatar uploads only after separate avatar-and-notes sync consent. The system photo picker accesses selected images, not the entire library; capture dates may be used to check diary dates. Images may be compressed and previews generated; saved avatars and journal images do not copy original location metadata. Package text recognition uses Apple Vision on device and can be inaccurate. Notifications are requested when you set reminders. Optional lock-screen displays may be visible to others.

5. Backups and exports

App data, including profiles, health records, diaries, photos, drafts and cloud cache, is excluded from system iCloud backup. Do not rely on system backup after uninstalling, changing devices or device failure. Export or back up explicitly and store files safely. A full backup may contain sensitive records, photos and recently deleted items, and has no additional app password encryption. Files saved or shared elsewhere cannot be remotely recalled; check file size and overwrite restrictions before restoring.

6. Retention and security

Local records remain until deleted. Permitted synced fields and separately enabled avatars and notes may also remain in your private or shared iCloud space. Stopping sync, signing out or switching to local storage does not automatically delete cloud content. Recently deleted items remain until permanently cleared. System storage protections are used but absolute security cannot be guaranteed; protect your device and backups.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You can view, edit, export and delete records in the app. Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Deleting local data does not delete originals in Photos, exports, backups, cloud records or snapshots. When enabled, changes and deletions of permitted shared content, including separately enabled avatars and notes, may sync after network delays; check cloud receipts. Delete cloud data using the iCloud sync page and confirm receipts. Removing a member alone does not invalidate the invitation link: stop the entire family care share to invalidate the old link. Removal or stopping cannot erase copies, screenshots or exports others already saved.

8. Support and privacy requests

Contact lyriclyteam@gmail.com for support, guardian or privacy requests. Emails, descriptions and attachments you voluntarily send are used to handle your request and retained as needed; you may request deletion after resolution except where retention is legally required. Please do not send Apple passwords, complete baby profiles, health records, sensitive photos or full backups.

9. Updates

Updates will be dated on this page. New uses, third-party services, developer-readable services or changes to sharing scope will be explained and consent sought where required. Reading this policy does not enable sync or permissions.
