Support

Local storage and sync

Cloud sync and family care sharing are optional. Baby avatars and basic care notes sync only after separate consent; shared-family members can see them. Notes may contain health information and are not keyword-filtered. Health facts, measurements, diaries, handover messages and journal photos remain local. Family cloud albums are unavailable.

Contact

Email lyriclyteam@gmail.com with your app version, device model, system version, steps and the error message. Mask names and sensitive details in screenshots. Never send Apple passwords, health records or a complete backup. Support is not an emergency service.

Where are records stored?

The default space is local. After explicitly choosing an iCloud account space and reopening the app, eligible new records enter the sync queue. Existing local records, photos and drafts do not automatically migrate. Check My → Data Storage and iCloud Sync for scope and status; queued does not mean confirmed.

How do I move to a new device?

App data is excluded from system iCloud backup. Before uninstalling or changing devices, create and safely save an explicit backup from My → Backup & Restore. For an iCloud account space, sign in to the same Apple account and verify sync. Only permitted structured fields and separately enabled avatars and basic care notes sync; health, measurements, diaries, handovers and journal photos do not. Limited snapshots exclude avatars and notes.

Can family members see records?

Create a family care share or accept an invitation and review its scope and permissions. The shared content includes permitted profiles and structured care records; separately enabled avatars and basic care notes may also be visible. A valid link can be forwarded, and members can edit or delete records. Sending an invitation does not mean joining is complete.

How do I delete data?

Confirm permanent deletion carefully. Local deletion does not delete Photos originals, backups, exports, cloud records or snapshots. Manage cloud data in iCloud Sync and verify receipts. To invalidate an invitation link, stop the entire share; copies others saved cannot be recalled.

Sync, photos or reminders not working?

Check your iCloud sign-in, network, available storage, sync errors, notification permission and Focus settings as relevant. Keep local records and drafts; do not repeatedly uninstall. For help, email a redacted error message without the invitation link or full backup.
